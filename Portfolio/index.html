Answers to common questions about features, background streaming, sound quality, and installation.

Yes, 100% free and ad-free! Vibentra is an open-source high-fidelity music streaming ecosystem. It lets you discover, search, and stream millions of tracks across JioSaavn, YouTube Music, and global charts with zero subscription fees and no annoying interruptions.

In version 1.4.2, every single album and playlist connects directly to live provider endpoints (JioSaavn and YouTube Music). When you open an album or curated playlist, Vibentra fetches its authentic tracklist instead of duplicate sample songs.

When you delete a playlist, its ID is permanently recorded in a deletion blacklist and purged from Cloud Firestore. Even when Firebase sync runs or you restart the app, the deleted playlist will never reappear.

Yes! Vibentra is engineered with active WebAudio keep-alive threading, MediaSession API integration, and native Capacitor Android background audio services. Your music continues streaming flawlessly even when your device screen is locked, in pocket, or when switching between other apps.
